My dearest Boo,

Boo, honestly I don't even know where to start. There's just so much. You are a doctor. You spend your days carrying other people's pain, holding it together when everything is falling apart around you. And then you come home, or I come pick you up, and you're still you. Still warm. Still mine. That still gets me every single time. My bujji boo moley, I see you. All of you.

The way you look at me sometimes. The way you just... adore me, like I'm something worth adoring. It makes me want to be better. Not for anyone else, just for you. You make me want to be the version of myself that actually deserves to be loved by you. That's real.

And Stangendhra. 😄 Our car, our little world on wheels. Those late nights and early mornings, just driving to the hospital or back, the city quiet, no agenda, just us talking nonsense or saying nothing at all. I love those drives more than I know how to say. Even just sitting there doing nothing together feels like something. I'd do a thousand of those drives and never get tired of it.

And yes, we fight. Let's just say it. We fight. 😂 But then we always find our way back, and somehow we end up laughing at the whole thing, going "wait, why were we even mad?" and turning it into a bit. That's us. That's what I love about us. We don't let things sit heavy for long. We choose each other again, every single time.

Your laugh, your cheeks, those dimples. That look you give me sometimes, that little lip thing you do like you know exactly what you're doing. You are so effortlessly beautiful and I'm not sure you fully realise it. But I do. I notice everything.

And I miss the chai, Boo. I really do. Coming back from work and you just having it ready, like it was nothing. That small thing meant more than I probably ever told you. It felt like home. You feel like home.

I want to marry you, Boo. I want to grow old with you. Grey hair(already grey only pa), old inside jokes, still roasting each other over evening chai about some fight we had years ago. I want all of it, every ordinary, messy, wonderful bit of it, as long as it's with you.

Thank you for loving me. Thank you for choosing me every day.💕

Forever yours, always and always.

Dudu
